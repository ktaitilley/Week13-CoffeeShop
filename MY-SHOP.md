The program is a coffeshop game loop where you can order food and drinks.
To run it: go in src/main/coffeeshop/coffeeshopApp and click the green buton next to Main to start.
Coffeeshop:builds the coffeeshop
CoffeshopApp: builds the game for the coffeeshop
Drink:builds drinks for the menu.
Food:builds food for the menu.
Menu:creates a menu for food and drinks.
MenuItem:adds food and drinks to menu.
Order: creates a list for the customers order.
One of the things i struggled with was making the loop for the game itself. i figured it out by making a variable using readnumber instead of putting it inside of the switch.
